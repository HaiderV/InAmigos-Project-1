InAmigos Foundation

Uniting Minds, Driving Meaningful Change.

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, we are a Section 8 registered non-profit organization committed to transforming vulnerable lives across India through collective action, education, healthcare, and sustainable empowerment.

Make A Donation Learn More
GET TO KNOW US BETTER

Local action, lasting change across India.

Our six initiatives address practical needs in different ways: Project Seva provides food and clothing, Bachpanshala supports children’s education, Jeev focuses on animal welfare, Udaan promotes women’s skills and financial independence, Prakriti advances environmental care, and Vikas builds employable skills. Volunteers, professionals, and corporate partners help bring this work to communities.

OUR MISSION IN ACTIVE COMMUNITIES

Providing Essential Resources, Transforming Vulnerable Lives.

InAmigos Foundation operates across India to address key societal challenges. Through our network of volunteers, we carry out ground-level initiatives focusing on education, hunger relief, and skill development.

Distributed over 50,000+ meals and essential clothing to underprivileged individuals and families across operational regions.

Project BACHPANSHALA
